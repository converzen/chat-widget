My Client Site — Voice Widget

Loads the voice-enhanced widget (cvz-widget-voice.js, global window.cvzVoiceWidget). Click the launcher, then the mic button in the input row. Speak; the stage pill shows listening / transcribing / thinking and the assistant's reply lands in the conversation. Typing still works in the same chat. Requires a cvz-chat with voice enabled, served over HTTPS or localhost (getUserMedia needs a secure context).
